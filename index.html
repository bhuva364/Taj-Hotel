Our Heritage

A Palace Restored, Not Reinvented

Once the residence of a merchant prince, Taj Hotel keeps its original jali screens, courtyard fountains and hand-painted ceilings intact — restored stone by stone rather than replaced. Every corridor still carries the scent of vetiver and old sandalwood.
